The Goal

Grow a small planet by gathering compatible matter from its orbit. There is no game over and no final stage: make calm, efficient merges and see how far your world can grow.

New orbs arrive continuously and drift around the whole field. Higher stages add more values, making valuable groups more rewarding and more difficult to arrange.

Capturing Matter

1. Hold and position

Press and hold anywhere on the field to make a capture ring. Keep holding and drag to move it over a useful group.

2. Match nearby values

A ring needs at least two normal orbs. Their numbers may differ by at most one: 1 + 2 works; 1 + 3 does not.

3. Keep holding

When the ring is valid, its bright arc fills and heats toward white as the hold completes. Releasing early changes nothing, and the ring stays open after a merge so you can chain the next group for a combo.

The full circle is the exact selection boundary. An orb counts when its center is inside it. A red ring means the selected values are incompatible.

Visible values

1234-6 Values unlock over the early stages. Once released, they remain possible forever.

Growth, Score, And Stages

Every merged orb adds hidden mass multiplied by its visible value to the planet. The same merge pays score, with a large bonus for collecting a bigger group at once. A high-value group of many orbs is exceptionally valuable.

Each new stage awards a power-up charge, makes future matter arrive a little faster, and speeds up orbital drift (up to 2x), so late runs never sit still. The planet takes on a new tinted identity each stage, with an orbit ring from stage 3 and a glow from stage 5. Stage advances briefly slow time and pulse the planet. The planet has a visual size limit, but its mass, stage, and score can continue forever.

Volatile Matter

✕ Dark volatile orbs are wildcards like stars: they can join a compatible normal group regardless of value, but a group with one still needs two normal orbs. They add no planet mass, shrink the payout by 25% each (multiplicatively), and break the combo streak. Merge quickly before they drift into the ring.

Combo Streak

Merges within 6 seconds of each other chain a combo that multiplies the payout, up to 5x at x5 and beyond. A chained merge shows its multiplier badge under the score popup and rings at a higher pitch. A volatile orb or a pause longer than 6 seconds resets the streak. Your best combo and capture accuracy are tracked behind the chart button in the game HUD.

Star Orbs

✦ Rare stars are wildcards: they can join a compatible normal group regardless of its value. A group with a star still needs two normal orbs, so one normal orb plus a star cannot merge.

Gold star

Grants one power-up charge.

Cyan star

Activates three expanded capture rings.

Violet star

Activates three focused capture rings.

Power-ups

Open the sparkle button in the game HUD to spend an earned charge. You begin a new run with one charge and gain more from stages and gold stars.

PowerEffect
PulsePushes every loose orb outward, opening room around the planet.
StabilizeGreatly slows orbital drift for seven seconds.
Expand fieldThe next three rings are wider, but each takes 0.5 seconds longer.
Focus fieldThe next three rings are tighter, but each takes 0.35 seconds less.
Thin fieldRemoves the outermost quarter of loose orbs.

Expanded and focused fields replace one another. Their three uses are only spent by completed captures, not canceled rings.

Settings

  • Sound controls the synthesized merge, star, stage, and power sounds.
  • Haptics provides a short vibration after successful merges where the device supports it.
  • Gentle start gives future new games fewer starting orbs and slower early spawning.
  • Capture hold time selects 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, or 2.0 seconds. The selected time locks when a capture begins.
  • Touch offset moves the effective ring in a compass direction, keeping it visible beside your finger. Set both direction and distance.
  • Unlimited power-ups allows every power to be used without charges.

Saving

Your active run, settings, and best score are saved locally in the browser. Leaving or closing the page preserves the field and resumes it next time. The simulation pauses while the browser tab is hidden, so time away never adds or moves matter.
